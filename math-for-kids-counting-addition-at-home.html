Math for kids at home: counting, numbers and early addition

Young children learn math most easily when they can see it, touch it and count it. Count real things first, then match them to numerals, and only then add and take away. This page gives a suggested order, ten-minute counting games for everyday life, and how Lumi Math Planet can carry part of the practice.

A suggested order

StepWhat your child doesAt home
1. CountingTouch and count one object at a timeCount spoons or stairs
2. NumeralsMatch a quantity to a numberPut 3 grapes next to the number 3
3. ComparingMore, fewer, the sameWhich plate has more apples?
4. Adding and taking awayPut in, take outYou have 4, eat 1, how many left?
5. PatternsSay what comes nextRed, blue, red, blue, …

These are general suggestions. When your child gets stuck, go back one step instead of pushing ahead.

Ten-minute counting games

  • Tidy-up counting: “Put five blocks back in the box.”
  • Grocery helper: let your child count oranges into the bag.
  • Finger addition: hold up three fingers, add two, and ask how many.
  • Pattern line: line up toys in a pattern and ask what comes next.
  • Clock check: point to the hour hand at mealtime and bedtime.

Stop while it is still fun, so your child wants to play again tomorrow.
