Free printable addition worksheets: sums to 5, 10 and 20

Three one-page worksheets for pre-K, kindergarten and 1st grade. Every time you open this page or tap New worksheets, you get a new mix of problems, so you can print a fresh set for tomorrow without your kid memorizing the answer sheet. No sign-up, nothing to download.

  • Picture sums to 5 — count the blue dots and the orange dots, then write the total. Good for kids who still count on their fingers.
  • Sums to 10 — 20 stacked problems. This is the set to repeat until the facts come quickly.
  • Sums to 20 — 20 stacked problems that cross ten, like 8 + 5 and 9 + 7.

Printing tips

  • Each worksheet prints on its own page (US Letter). The page header, buttons and links are left off the printout.
  • Answer keys print on separate pages, and only when the box above is checked. Leave it unchecked for the kids’ copy, then check it and print again for yours.
  • Want just one level? Use Print this sheet on that worksheet.
  • No printer handy? Choose Save as PDF in the print dialog.

Getting more out of a worksheet

  • Keep it short. One page a day beats three pages on Saturday. Stop while your child still wants to do one more.
  • Let them use tools. Fingers, a ten frame drawn on scrap paper, or a handful of cereal are all fine. Speed comes later.
  • Circle the slow ones. If 7 + 6 takes a while every time, that’s the fact to practice next week.
  • Making ten helps with sums to 20. For 8 + 5, move 2 from the 5 to make 10, then add the 3 that are left.
