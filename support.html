Troubleshooting

Close Lumi Math Planet completely, then open it again. Restart the iPhone or iPad to clear a temporary system problem. Open the App Store and install any available update for Lumi Math Planet. If the issue continues, include exact reproduction steps in your email.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Lumi Math Planet does not require a developer account or sign-in.

Can I use the core features offline?

Yes. Lumi Math Planet's core processing runs on the device. App downloads, purchases and explicitly online features still require a connection.

Does the app contain ads or third-party tracking?

No. Lumi Math Planet contains no third-party ads, analytics or cross-app tracking SDK.

How do notifications work?

Any reminders you enable are scheduled by the device. You can change or disable them in iOS Settings.

How do I update the app?

Open the App Store, tap your account picture and install any available update for Lumi Math Planet.

What should I include in a bug report?

Email hourstag.app@gmail.com with the device model, iOS version, app version, steps to reproduce and a screenshot that contains no private information.
