1. What it is

Lumi Math Planet: Explorer is a picture-based math app for young children: they solve questions that are read aloud, and every right answer lights up a little planet.

  • 46 picture-based question types, each one read aloud, from counting and addition to times tables, clocks and money
  • Right answers light up planets; wrong answers get gentle encouragement instead of a buzzer
  • Parent Zone that follows progress across five skill areas and opens focused practice on weak spots
  • Printable worksheets with answer keys and a learning report you can print or save as a PDF
  • Daily challenge and 44 interface languages

2. Who it is for

Children from pre-readers to those just starting school, and anyone rebuilding number foundations; every child starts at their own level. Parents get a Parent Zone to follow progress. No ads, no account, and every outbound link sits behind a parent gate.

3. How it is paid for

The download is free, and you can start with the first planet and the daily challenge without buying anything. A single one-time in-app purchase unlocks the whole galaxy. There is no subscription.

Frequently asked questions

Does my child need to read?

No. Every question is visual and read aloud.

Is there a subscription?

No. There is one one-time in-app purchase.

Can I use it without buying?

Yes. The first planet and the daily challenge are available to start with.

Are there ads or data collection?

No ads, and no data is collected; nothing leaves the device.

Can I print practice sheets?

Yes. The Parent Zone exports worksheets with answer keys and a learning report.
